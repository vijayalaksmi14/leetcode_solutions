## Problem: Reverse String (Easy)
**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/reverse-string/

### Approach
Used a two-pointer technique (one at the beginning, one at the end) swapping characters in-place until the pointers meet in the middle.

### Complexity
- Time: $O(n)$
- Space: $O(1)$

### Notes
- Performed in-place modification without allocating extra memory for another array.
